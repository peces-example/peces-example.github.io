# PECES-initiative

PECES is a workshop on the effective sharing and teaching of coding practices and the use of large open-source data. This workshop will equip participants to confidently incorporate best coding practices into their curricula, bringing the most remote areas of the world into the classroom! Chosen applicants will travel to the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, where the workshop will take place, and spend three days in structured interactive coding sessions and loosely structured brainstorming, networking, and social sessions. 

Focus areas of the interactive coding sessions will include (1) best practices for introducing programming to Earth, ocean, and atmospheric science students, (2) effectively accessing and analyzing large open-source data and models in classroom environments, and (3) sharing and compiling resources for teaching.
